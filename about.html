About InfraCode Solutions

Enterprise infrastructure consultancy with certified engineers and automation-first delivery.

Our Story

InfraCode Solutions was established by infrastructure engineers with hands-on experience deploying Red Hat OpenShift and Nutanix platforms for enterprises across the Middle East, India and Southeast Asia.

The pattern was always the same: enterprises needed automation-first infrastructure, but got inconsistent results from different vendors. InfraCode was built to solve that — bringing certified, hands-on expertise directly to enterprises, with automation built in from day one.

What We Believe

Infrastructure should be consistent, auditable and operationally sustainable. Every deployment we build is designed with automation-first principles — not as an afterthought, but as the foundation.

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all. Every engagement starts with understanding your specific environment, goals and constraints — then designing a solution that fits.
